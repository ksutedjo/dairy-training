Example line: mozzarella, milk to pack

Operators learn where their job sits on the line, and what goes wrong when a step is skipped. Sample flow, not a validated process.

  1. Intake

    Raw milk received and checked.

  2. Pasteurise

    72 °C · 15 s

    Heat treatment of the milk.

  3. Set and cut

    Coagulate, then cut the curd.

  4. Cook and drain

    Whey drawn off.

  5. Stretch

    Heat, stretch, mould.

  6. Brine

    Cool and salt.

  7. Pack

    Label and seal.

Project 1

The new operator game In development

The first version covers what a new operator needs in the first weeks. Each level is a short scenario with a clear right and wrong, and a reason.

First version: week-one topics

Six topics, each played in short scenario rounds.

  • Personal hygiene and GMPHands, clothing, jewellery, illness
  • Hygiene zonesWhere you may go, and what you carry
  • Handling and cross-contaminationTools, hoses, floors, product
  • CIP basicsWhat cleaning-in-place is and why it matters
  • Chemicals and safetyLabels, dilution, protective gear
  • Reporting problemsWhat to say, to whom, how fast

Design goals

Made for the plant floor, not the office.

  • Phone firstPlays on low-cost phones.
  • Light on dataSmall downloads, usable on weak connections.
  • QR at the lineScan a code on the wall to open the right level.
  • Pictures and audioLess reading, so more people can learn from it.
  • Two languagesBahasa Indonesia and English.
